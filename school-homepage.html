Our Story

More Than an Education — A Foundation for Life

Founded in 1962, Westbrook Academy has grown into one of the region's most distinguished institutions for middle and high school students. Our curriculum blends rigorous academics with enriching arts, athletics, and community engagement.

We believe every student deserves an environment where curiosity is rewarded, potential is nurtured, and character is shaped with intention.
